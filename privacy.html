This policy explains what TradeLens collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can request deletion. TradeLens shows crypto trading signals with simulated paper-trading results. It does not place real trades.

1. Data we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and Google user ID, provided by Google when you sign in.
  • App usage data: the signals, paper trades and settings you use inside the app.
  • Demo trading keys: if you choose to connect a Binance Futures testnet key, the key and secret are stored encrypted on our server. They are used only to place Demo orders for your account.

2. Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your paper-trading results and settings.
  • To run Demo trades on your behalf, only if you have turned that on.

3. Where it is stored and who processes it

Account and app data is stored in Supabase. The signal engine runs on a server that reads public market data and writes signals to the same database. We use Google for sign-in. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Live trading

Live trading is not enabled in TradeLens. All trades shown in the app are simulated, and no real funds are used.

5. How long we keep data

Signal records are kept for a limited period. Account data is kept while your account is active. Stored Demo keys are kept until you disconnect them or ask us to delete them.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can sign out at any time, disconnect a Demo key in the app, or request deletion of your account and data. To request deletion, message us on Telegram: https://t.me/gwkav7wo1

7. Children

TradeLens is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
