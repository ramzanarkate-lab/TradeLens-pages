Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what the TradeLens app collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account name, email address and Google user ID.
  • If you use the app as a guest: an anonymous account ID. No name or email.
  • App usage data needed to run the service: for example your paper-trading history, your settings and your notification choices.

We do not collect your Google password. We do not collect payment card details in the app.

Why we collect it

To sign you in, to keep your account, and to show you your own paper-trading data and settings. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your account and paper-trading data are stored in Supabase, the database and sign-in service we use. Google sign-in tokens are handled by Google and Supabase. The app does not store your Google password, and we do not keep your tokens in our own systems.

Sharing

We share data only with the services needed to run TradeLens: Google (to sign you in) and Supabase (to store your account and data). We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. Email [YOUR-EMAIL] from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it.

Not financial advice

TradeLens shows signals and simulated paper trades with virtual money. It is for information only. It is not financial advice, and no real trades are made on your behalf.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
